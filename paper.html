Morality at the Box Office:
A Narrative Economics Analysis, 1977–2025

Cédric Rötheli

Bachelor Thesis
University of Bern
2026

This thesis examines how the narrative structures of commercially successful films have changed over the past five decades. It studies the 20 highest-grossing films worldwide in each year from 1977 to 2025 and uses plot synopses and subtitle material to compare their narrative structure across time.

The thesis contains the conceptual framework, data and sample construction, LLM-based narrative coding, validation, empirical results, and discussion and limitations.
